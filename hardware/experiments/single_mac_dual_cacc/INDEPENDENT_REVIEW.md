# Independent acceptance check

From this worktree, `bash hardware/experiments/single_mac_dual_cacc/run.sh` exited 0 on 2026-10-19 after the port-budget correction. The rerun passed the unit test (reset, page ownership, latency, backpressure and clear), tiny/ragged/full integer output comparisons against independent NumPy golden values, and two additional backpressure runs. Full FFN1 produced 25 344 exact INT32 outputs in 125 603 cycles. The unchanged one-core baseline took 154 240 cycles and the old two-core dual-page harness took 125 603. Yosys elaboration reported 16 `$mul` cells in the new module, 16 in the old one-core module and 32 in the two-core wrapper.

Review correction: the active page receives 16 products per cycle, so CACC requires 16 parallel row updates. The former "one write port per page" macro estimate was incorrect. The current RTL stores both pages in registers (4 096 data flops plus 512 valid bits); a legal SRAM design needs explicit row banking, old-sum read/forwarding and port timing. A 64 × `16b×128` bank sketch overallocates capacity and is not a mapped or synthesized design. Area, power, 150 MHz timing and E203 integration remain unproven. Generated path-dependent logs were restored after the rerun; the committed worktree is clean.
